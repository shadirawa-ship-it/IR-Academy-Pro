## Critical Safety Rules

### 1. Clinical Responsibility
**All clinical decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.**

- Attending physicians must make all patient-specific decisions
- Institutional protocols take absolute precedence
- Never rely on this app for clinical guidance
- Always use current manufacturer Instructions For Use (IFU)
- Follow your institution's credentialing and competency verification

### 2. Device Compatibility Checker
**The Device Compatibility Checker is INFORMATIONAL ONLY.**

⚠️ **What It Does:**
- Checks basic geometric fit between device combinations
- Confirms physical size compatibility
- References standard manufacturer specifications

⚠️ **What It Does NOT Do:**
- Does NOT approve device combinations
- Does NOT guarantee clinical safety
- Does NOT replace device IFU instructions
- Does NOT account for patient-specific anatomy
- Does NOT perform clinical risk assessment

**You MUST:**
1. Verify all combinations with current manufacturer IFU
2. Consult your institution's biomedical engineering team
3. Confirm compatibility with your specific device lots
4. Assess patient-specific factors (vessel size, anatomy, pathology)

### 3. Procedural Instructions
**All procedural content is educational reference material only.**

⚠️ Procedural steps are:
- Based on published guidelines and literature
- Generalized descriptions (not patient-specific)
- Not intended as step-by-step clinical instructions
- Subject to individual variation and institutional protocols

**You MUST:**
1. Undergo formal procedural training with qualified mentors
2. Follow your institution's standard operating procedures (SOPs)
3. Maintain current competency and certification
4. Document all procedures per institutional requirements
5. Report adverse events and complications appropriately

### 4. Device Materials
**Materials specifications are informational references only.**

⚠️ Materials Catalog information:
- Based on manufacturer published specifications
- May not reflect current product variations
- Subject to change without notice
- Not a warranty or guarantee

**You MUST:**
1. Check all materials against current product packaging
2. Verify lot numbers and expiration dates
3. Consult with biomedical teams on compatibility
4. Report any material defects to manufacturers
5. Maintain device traceability per institutional policy

### 5. Anatomical Information
**Anatomical illustrations are generalized educational diagrams.**

⚠️ Anatomy is:
- Schematic and simplified (not photorealistic)
- Representative of typical anatomy
- Does NOT show individual variation
- Does NOT account for pathological anatomy
- Educational reference only

**You MUST:**
1. Review patient-specific imaging (angiography, CTA, MRA, ultrasound)
2. Assess individual anatomical variation
3. Consider pathological changes and disease
4. Use advanced imaging for surgical planning
5. Adapt procedures to patient-specific anatomy

## Data Safety & Privacy

**This application collects no data at all.** It is a single static HTML file with no backend, no analytics SDK, and no native app. An earlier version of this document described module-usage tracking, device/OS reporting, crash reporting, and Android-specific at-rest encryption — none of that infrastructure exists, and the description has been removed rather than corrected in place, since it did not describe this product.

The only thing stored anywhere is your theme preference and current tab, saved locally in your own browser and never transmitted.
